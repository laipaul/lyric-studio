# Lyrics: timing, display and effects

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). Alignment, vocal-start check, hand-set starts, hints, safe zones, lead, effects, long lines, hooks. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Automatic lyric timing: `pnpm align` (Demucs htdemucs vocals + WhisperX wav2vec2 forced alignment, en/fr) writes enhanced LRC (line stamps + <mm:ss.xx> word tags), keeps hooks and pauses, flags lines below 0.5 confidence. Acceptance rule: the first sung word of every song, and the first word after every long instrumental, must be placed right by default (within ~0.3 s) with no manual fix. Vocal-start check (pnpm make, a post-process on the stored alignment, never re-aligning; a full re-alignment only with --realign), for line 1, every line after an instrumental gap (>= 4 s without voice, in the vocal stem or the alignment), every line whose first word lasts >= 2 s and every hook line (an early karaoke highlight is very visible; `pnpm lyrics:check-start --all` checks every line): three signals, (a) voice = the onset of sustained voice (>= 0.5 s, at most 15 dB under the song's singing level measured on the aligned lines, so bleed, breaths and short hums do not count; the previous line's own singing (the stretch its last word is in) never counts, and a line sung on without a pause has no voice signal), (b) words = a short speech-recognition pass (services/audio first_words: faster-whisper "small" on the cached vocal stem, only in windows around those lines, cached in .studio/lyrics/words-<key>.json; skipped with a note without the AI packages) matched against the line's first words (case, punctuation ignored; a missed short first word allowed), (c) word durations = the alignment's own first word: stretched -> the singing-level energy just before the second word, else the stamp. The aligner's stamp is a signal too; word durations count only when the first word is stretched (else they are the stamp). A line is never moved on one signal alone: a move needs two signals within 0.3 s (the stamp when it is one of them, else voice onset, then a stretched word's energy, then the recognised word), except a first word of >= 4 s with the recognised words within 1.0 s of the word-duration estimate -> the recognised words (the voice onset when within 0.3 s of them), and a first word of >= 2 s with voice and words both later than the stamp and within 0.6 s of each other -> the recognised words (not on the last line: a held note). One signal disagreeing with the stamp by at least the brand's lead-in (motion.gapLeadInS, 1 s; smaller differences are invisible to the viewer: plain "in time") and nothing else agreeing -> the stamp stays and the line is "flagged for review" (printed as "FLAGGED FOR REVIEW", listed at the end, a QA WARN "Line starts to review", for the Lyrics tab); a stretched first word on a hook or the last line may be a held note: flagged, never moved on that alone. Moves of more than 0.3 s, never onto the next line; a later start inside the previous line (two signals agree) shortens that line's end to the new start (its last note was held too long); an earlier start inside the previous line is flagged. The printout also shows the vocal track's level at the stamp against the voice level (why the voice was or was not heard there). pnpm make always prints per checked line the stamp, each signal, the decision and why ("voice, words, word durations agree"), plus "guard: line 1 moved from 0:03.2 to 0:11.8 (vocals start)" per move (and "line N now ends there" when the previous line was shortened); the same is in guard-<lrc hash>.json and the manifest (lyrics.vocal_guard, lyrics.vocal_check). The voice map (services/audio voice_map: the vocal stem's level every 20 ms; Demucs only when the stem is not cached, never WhisperX) is cached in .studio/lyrics/voice-<song hash>.v2.json; the stored LRC is never changed. `pnpm lyrics:check-start "<song folder>"` prints the same check without making videos. QA "Lines start with the vocals" WARNs for a line still more than 1 s before its voice. AI packages are an optional extra (`pnpm audio:setup --ai`; PyTorch 2.8 cu128 on Windows); models live in services/audio/models.
- Hand-set line starts (Lyrics tab) live in the song's hints.lrc as "[mm:ss.xx]! #<line> <text>" lines: the aligner never sees them (it gets the file without them, byte for byte the taps; a hints.lrc with only such lines counts as no hints), so the stored alignment is kept; pnpm make (and check-start) moves that line as a whole to that start (squeezed before the next line, a line whose text changed or that would pass a neighbour is ignored with a message), records it in the manifest (lyrics.hand_times), and the vocal-start check leaves those lines alone. The Assets tab's hints check does not count them as taps.
- Hints (tapped times) are remembered per project: the first --hint is stored with the project and reused on every later alignment ("using stored hints from …"); aligning without them (--no-hint, or a lost copy) warns clearly. A lyrics.txt change in hook markers ("* ") only does not re-align: the stored alignment is reused and the hook flags follow the current lyrics.txt.
- Lyrics stay out of each platform's UI: never inside its safe zones (presets/platforms.json, fractions of the frame per side). The vertical position is computed from those safe zones: centred in the usable area, slightly above its middle (lyricsAt, default 0.45) — never a fixed percentage.
- Lyric lines appear 0.25 s before their stamp (lead, configurable), plus an optional lyrics offset; no early cut.
- Lyric effects (packages/video/src/effects, Remotion interpolate/spring/Easing only, no GSAP or Framer Motion): each effect is a registered module (duration, easing, intensity subtle | balanced | expressive), per line or per word (aligned word timings, else words spread from the line's stamp). Effects: glide (default verses: Apple Music-style scroll, old line up ~40 px and out, next line rises from small/faded to full in ~500 ms ease-in-out), cinematic-fade, soft-blur (max 4 px), dreamy-glow, word-reveal, karaoke-highlight (default hooks: the sung word in the brand accent colour), floating, typewriter. Every line is settled (fully readable) at its appearance time, 0.25 s before its stamp; transitions shorten when lines come fast. Chosen through the recipe: brand.json (motion.verseEffect / motion.hookEffect, and brand.json perPlatform), the song's song.json (default and perPlatform), then per run (--line-effect / --hook-effect, "name[:intensity]"). platforms.json is format only and never sets effects (an old "motion" entry there is ignored with a message). Every effect list shows a hint in one format ("glide · verses", "karaoke-highlight · hooks", ...) and marks the brand default. showNextLine per platform (default true): the next line waits small underneath. Before an instrumental pause the last line fades out 1 s after its last word ends (0.4 s fade) instead of staying on screen. During an instrumental gap longer than brand.json motion.gapHideS (default 3 s; from the last sung word, or the section start, to the next first word) no lyric is shown at all, not even the waiting next line; the next line fades in motion.gapLeadInS (default 1 s) before its first sung word and is settled at its usual time (manifests without lyricEffects.gap keep the old timing). QA's safe-zone box includes each effect's movement and the waiting next line. Manifests without lyricEffects render with the old fade/pop. "pnpm effects:preview <project>" renders all effects side by side (labelled 1920x1080 grid) on 2-3 real lines around the first hook; --effect <name> renders one effect full-size in 9:16 and 16:9.
- Long lyric lines: one line if it fits, else two balanced lines, then shrink up to 25%. Only as an absolute last resort three lines (never more), with a warning naming the line ("Line 3 is too long for phone screens; split it in lyrics.txt") from pnpm tap and render:test.
- Hook lines: "* " at the start of a line in lyrics.txt or after the time stamp in the .lrc; every line with the same words is a hook too. Hook lines can use their own font.
- Effects added 30 Oct 2026 (from the user's Canva finishing touches; src/effects/spread.ts, typewriter.ts, typing.ts, breathe.ts): **typewriter** is now letter by letter (typingPlan: from the moment the line appears (after the previous line has gone), one letter at a time at a steady pace = the intensity (subtle ~8, balanced ~14, expressive ~21 letters/s, TYPE_BASE_CPS 14 x the amount), raised just enough that every word is fully typed when it is sung (TYPE_MAX_CPS 90); a thin cursor in the accent colour after the last typed letter (takes no room), blinking softly (1.1 s) once the line is typed; fades out); **typewriter-erase**: the same, leaving by erasing its letters right to left over its exit while it dims; **typewriter-words**: the first typewriter (each word typed in 0.2 s before it is sung). Old manifests with "typewriter" render letter by letter now. **spread**: letters gathered close (-0.12 em, balanced) open up to their spacing while the line fades in (700 ms, ease-out), gather a little as it fades out; spacing never above 0, so never wider than the laid-out line. Title cards (Textboard): typewriter cards type at the calm pace and are complete a second before they end (typedShare), erase on the way out with typewriter-erase; spread opens their letter spacing. **Breathe** (recipe "breathe" off | gentle | beat, per platform, Style tab; props lyricEffects.breathe { mode, downbeats (video frames of the song's detected downbeats) }): an add-on to every effect, the line's scale x (1 + depth x (1 + cos) / 2), depth 2% verses / 3.5% hooks, "gentle" every 4 s, "beat" once a bar with the peak on each downbeat; not on the waiting next line; QA's safe-zone box counts it (effectExtents x BREATHE_MAX_SCALE). Style tab: "Try an effect" preview (components/effect-preview.tsx): the song's first three lines (StyleData.sampleLines) drawn by LyricScene in a @remotion/player loop with any effect, intensity (shown as Typing pace calm / normal / quick for the typewriters) and Breathe; trying saves nothing.
- Effects added 30 Oct 2026, step 1 of the user's six (src/effects/clarify.ts, ascend.ts, ink.ts): **clarify** (word effect, moves like the verse effect): words still to come wait dim (35%) and softly blurred (4 px x the amount, at most 5), each pulls into focus over the 8 frames before it is sung (settled when sung). **ascend** (line effect, swap): while the line comes in (900 ms), its letters rise into place one after another (LineLook wave 0 -> 1; ascendLetter: 5 letters moving at once, from 28 px x the amount below, ease-out), in a wave across all its rows; settled at the usual time; fades out; QA's box counts the letters below (LyricEffect reach). **ink** (line effect, swap): uncovered left to right behind a soft-edged mask (inkMask, 12% edge), row after row, at the typewriter's pace x 1.3 (every word uncovered by the time it is sung); fades out. Title cards: ascend letters rise (WaveText), ink uncovers the card at the calm pace (done a second before it ends), clarify fades (no word times).
- Music-reactive add-ons, 30 Oct 2026 (src/effects/accents.ts; recipe heldGlow / chorusLift / shine "off" (default) | "on", per platform, Style tab with "Use for all my songs" = brand.json perPlatform on every platform with lyrics (setBrandAllPlatforms; the song's own value removed); props lyricEffects heldGlow / chorusLift / shine / downbeats; on any effect, never on the waiting next line): **held-note glow**: a word with its own aligned time of >= 0.8 s glows in the accent colour (up to 0.75; text tinted 35% toward the accent) rising over 70% of the hold, settling over 20 frames after; **chorus lift**: the first line of each chorus (a hook line after a non-hook) scales up to +5% with +0.3 glow, rising while it comes in, full when settled, eased back over 45 frames (QA's box counts it on hook lines); **shine**: one light sweep (20 frames, a white band clipped to the text) across each chorus line on the first downbeat at or after it is settled (else 15 frames after), not while a typewriter is still typing. "Try an effect" has the three as checkboxes (the sample lines hold their last word ~1 s).
