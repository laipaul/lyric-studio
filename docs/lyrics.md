# Lyrics: timing, display and effects

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). Alignment, vocal-start check, hand-set starts, hints, safe zones, lead, effects, long lines, hooks. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Automatic lyric timing: `pnpm align` (Demucs htdemucs vocals + WhisperX wav2vec2 forced alignment, en/fr) writes enhanced LRC (line stamps + <mm:ss.xx> word tags), keeps hooks and pauses, flags lines below 0.5 confidence. Acceptance rule: the first sung word of every song, and the first word after every long instrumental, must be placed right by default (within ~0.3 s) with no manual fix. Vocal-start check (pnpm make, a post-process on the stored alignment, never re-aligning; a full re-alignment only with --realign), for line 1, every line after an instrumental gap (>= 4 s without voice, in the vocal stem or the alignment), every line whose first word lasts >= 2 s and every hook line (an early karaoke highlight is very visible; `pnpm lyrics:check-start --all` checks every line): three signals, (a) voice = the onset of sustained voice (>= 0.5 s, at most 15 dB under the song's singing level measured on the aligned lines, so bleed, breaths and short hums do not count; the previous line's own singing (the stretch its last word is in) never counts, and a line sung on without a pause has no voice signal), (b) words = a short speech-recognition pass (services/audio first_words: faster-whisper "small" on the cached vocal stem, only in windows around those lines, cached in .studio/lyrics/words-<key>.json; skipped with a note without the AI packages) matched against the line's first words (case, punctuation ignored; a missed short first word allowed), (c) word durations = the alignment's own first word: stretched -> the singing-level energy just before the second word, else the stamp. The aligner's stamp is a signal too; word durations count only when the first word is stretched (else they are the stamp). A line is never moved on one signal alone: a move needs two signals within 0.3 s (the stamp when it is one of them, else voice onset, then a stretched word's energy, then the recognised word), except a first word of >= 4 s with the recognised words within 1.0 s of the word-duration estimate -> the recognised words (the voice onset when within 0.3 s of them), and a first word of >= 2 s with voice and words both later than the stamp and within 0.6 s of each other -> the recognised words (not on the last line: a held note). One signal disagreeing with the stamp by at least the brand's lead-in (motion.gapLeadInS, 1 s; smaller differences are invisible to the viewer: plain "in time") and nothing else agreeing -> the stamp stays and the line is "flagged for review" (printed as "FLAGGED FOR REVIEW", listed at the end, a QA WARN "Line starts to review", for the Lyrics tab); a stretched first word on a hook or the last line may be a held note: flagged, never moved on that alone. Moves of more than 0.3 s, never onto the next line; a later start inside the previous line (two signals agree) shortens that line's end to the new start (its last note was held too long); an earlier start inside the previous line is flagged. The printout also shows the vocal track's level at the stamp against the voice level (why the voice was or was not heard there). pnpm make always prints per checked line the stamp, each signal, the decision and why ("voice, words, word durations agree"), plus "guard: line 1 moved from 0:03.2 to 0:11.8 (vocals start)" per move (and "line N now ends there" when the previous line was shortened); the same is in guard-<lrc hash>.json and the manifest (lyrics.vocal_guard, lyrics.vocal_check). The voice map (services/audio voice_map: the vocal stem's level every 20 ms; Demucs only when the stem is not cached, never WhisperX) is cached in .studio/lyrics/voice-<song hash>.v2.json; the stored LRC is never changed. `pnpm lyrics:check-start "<song folder>"` prints the same check without making videos. QA "Lines start with the vocals" WARNs for a line still more than 1 s before its voice. AI packages are an optional extra (`pnpm audio:setup --ai`; PyTorch 2.8 cu128 on Windows); models live in services/audio/models.
- Voice source (L1a, 31 Oct 2026): everything that listens to the voice (pnpm align, the voice map, first_words, the vocal-start check, QA "Lines start with the vocals") uses the vocal stem when the song folder has one (any file name, linked in .studio\files.json, else vocals.wav; downloaded untouched from Suno; the song is Suno's mix trimmed in a DAW), else the vocals the app separates (Demucs, the fallback). The stem is checked against the song once per song and stem content (services/audio app/vocal_stem.py, analysis only; result in .studio\lyrics\stem-check-<song>-<stem>.json and workspace\cache\stems\match-*.json): both resampled to 8 kHz mono, GCC-PHAT cross-correlation in the voice band (150 Hz - 3.8 kHz), one offset for the whole song (stem time = song time + offset), then measured again on 5 stretches with voice from start to end (12 s each); every stretch must find a clear peak (>= 12 standard deviations; unrelated audio scores 5-7) within 50 ms of the whole-song offset, and the stem may miss at most 10 s of the song. Then a lined-up copy on the song's timeline (16 kHz mono, only the overlapping part, silence elsewhere) is written to workspace\cache\stems\<song hash>-suno-<stem hash 12>-<offset>ms-vocals.wav and used instead of the Demucs stem; "Vocal stem lined up: 1.8 s offset, from trimming the song's start." A constant speed difference (the DAW export plays the master e.g. 0.2 % faster or slower than the stem, so the offset creeps along the song and later stretches smear) is measured and corrected, not rejected: only when the plain check fails and the whole-song peak is clear, the speed f (0.99-1.01, coarse 0.001 then fine 0.0001 steps, best whole-song peak; |f-1| < 0.0002 counts as none) is searched on an analysis copy of the stem (warped[x] = stem[f x]) and the same check runs on it; stem time = offset + speed x song time, the lined-up copy follows it, the file name gets "-x<speed 5 decimals>" and the voice key "-x<speed>" only then, the label is "vocal stem, offset 0.7 s, speed corrected", the message adds "the song plays 0.2 % faster than the stem (from the export), corrected for the lyric timing." (check version 2: .studio\lyrics\stem-check-<song>-<stem>.v2.json and match-*.v2.json, so an earlier rejection is checked again). Otherwise (a part cut or repeated in the middle, a tempo change that is not constant or beyond 1 %, another version, a silent or much shorter stem) the plain message "This vocal stem doesn't follow the song: probably another version of the song, or the song was edited in the middle." and Demucs. Any sample rate (resampled for analysis only). The audio scripts take --stem <file>|none|auto (auto: the stem linked in the song folder's .studio\files.json, else vocals.wav next to the song); pnpm make passes it explicitly (src/node/voice-source.ts). Caches: the stored alignment's key adds "stem-<stem hash 12>-<offset>ms" only for the stem (Demucs keys unchanged, so nothing aligned before is redone), the voice map is voice-<song hash>-stem-<...>.v2.json, the recognised words' key includes it too. backing-vocals.wav is never used for alignment (nor for anything yet). The manifest records lyrics.voice_source {kind, label "vocal stem, offset 1.8 s" / "separated by the app", offset_ms, stem_sha256, message} (no paths), the alignment report has "voice", the vocal-start check's record has check.voice, and pnpm make prints "lyrics: voice: …". Adding or replacing vocals.wav in the Assets tab (WAV only; the old one to .studio\replaced\) checks it at once and queues a "Lyric timing · vocal stem" run (make_run options lyricsOnly: alignment + hand-set starts + vocal-start check, no videos; src/node/lyric-timing.ts), so it re-aligns automatically with the stored hints and hand-set starts kept; the Lyrics tab shows the Demucs alignment until the stem's is stored. Prepare's to-do: "Add the vocal stem for better lyric timing" (never blocking), or the check's message when the stem is not used. `pnpm lyrics:compare-voice "<song folder>"` aligns on both sources (once each, stored) and prints them side by side (start, confidence, check result per line; totals of lines flagged for review, low-confidence lines, lines moved by the check), also saved to .studio\lyrics\compare-voice-<time>.md. Vocal-start rules on a clean stem (tests in vocal-guard.test.ts): unchanged thresholds, two changes the tests showed were needed for a wet stem: a pause of >= 100 ms under the voice threshold followed by an attack of >= 12 dB within 60 ms starts a new voice stretch even when the reverb tail fills the pause (only when both parts last >= 0.5 s), and the stretch a line is in is the one going on at its stamp, else one starting within 0.2 s of it, before one that merely ends within 0.2 s (a reverb tail ending just before a stamp is not that line's voice).
- The vocal stem's timing is the base (decided by the user, 31 Oct 2026): hand-set starts are the user's corrections on top of it, and every line can go back to the stem's timing (↺ per line, "Reset all to the vocal stem's timing" for all, both with Undo). Hand-set starts made before the song had a vocal stem were corrections of the old (Demucs) timing: the first time the timing on the stem is used (Lyrics tab, Save, pnpm make, the lyric-timing run) they are reset once (src/node/hand-reset.ts): the "!" lines leave hints.lrc (the taps stay byte for byte, so nothing re-aligns), the old hints.lrc is kept in .studio\replaced\, the log says how many, and the Lyrics tab shows a notice with Undo (the starts come back and then count as made on the stem). Which voice the starts were made on is kept in .studio\hand-times.json ({voice: "demucs" | "stem-<hash12>-<offset>ms", reset?}); starts saved while the stem is used are never reset automatically (also not when the stem is replaced).
- Hand-set line starts (Lyrics tab) live in the song's hints.lrc as "[mm:ss.xx]! #<line> <text>" lines: the aligner never sees them (it gets the file without them, byte for byte the taps; a hints.lrc with only such lines counts as no hints), so the stored alignment is kept; pnpm make (and check-start) moves that line as a whole to that start (squeezed before the next line, a line whose text changed or that would pass a neighbour is ignored with a message), records it in the manifest (lyrics.hand_times), and the vocal-start check leaves those lines alone. The Assets tab's hints check does not count them as taps.
- Hints (tapped times) are remembered per project: the first --hint is stored with the project and reused on every later alignment ("using stored hints from …"); aligning without them (--no-hint, or a lost copy) warns clearly. A lyrics.txt change in hook markers ("* ") only does not re-align: the stored alignment is reused and the hook flags follow the current lyrics.txt.
- Lyrics stay out of each platform's UI: never inside its safe zones (presets/platforms.json, fractions of the frame per side). The vertical position is computed from those safe zones: centred in the usable area, slightly above its middle (lyricsAt, default 0.45) — never a fixed percentage.
- Lyric lines appear 0.25 s before their stamp (lead, configurable), plus an optional lyrics offset; no early cut.
- Lyric effects (packages/video/src/effects, Remotion interpolate/spring/Easing only, no GSAP or Framer Motion): each effect is a registered module (duration, easing, intensity subtle | balanced | expressive), per line or per word (aligned word timings, else words spread from the line's stamp). Effects: glide (default verses: Apple Music-style scroll, old line up ~40 px and out, next line rises from small/faded to full in ~500 ms ease-in-out), cinematic-fade, soft-blur (max 4 px), dreamy-glow, word-reveal, karaoke-highlight (default hooks: the sung word in the brand accent colour), floating, typewriter. Every line is settled (fully readable) at its appearance time, 0.25 s before its stamp; transitions shorten when lines come fast. Chosen through the recipe: brand.json (motion.verseEffect / motion.hookEffect, and brand.json perPlatform), the song's song.json (default and perPlatform), then per run (--line-effect / --hook-effect, "name[:intensity]"). platforms.json is format only and never sets effects (an old "motion" entry there is ignored with a message). Every effect list shows a hint in one format ("glide · verses", "karaoke-highlight · hooks", ...) and marks the brand default. showNextLine per platform (default true): the next line waits small underneath. Before an instrumental pause the last line fades out 1 s after its last word ends (0.4 s fade) instead of staying on screen. During an instrumental gap longer than brand.json motion.gapHideS (default 3 s; from the last sung word, or the section start, to the next first word) no lyric is shown at all, not even the waiting next line; the next line fades in motion.gapLeadInS (default 1 s) before its first sung word and is settled at its usual time (manifests without lyricEffects.gap keep the old timing). QA's safe-zone box includes each effect's movement and the waiting next line. Manifests without lyricEffects render with the old fade/pop. "pnpm effects:preview <project>" renders all effects side by side (labelled 1920x1080 grid) on 2-3 real lines around the first hook; --effect <name> renders one effect full-size in 9:16 and 16:9.
- Long lyric lines: one line if it fits, else two balanced lines, then shrink up to 25%. Only as an absolute last resort three lines (never more), with a warning naming the line ("Line 3 is too long for phone screens; split it in lyrics.txt") from pnpm tap and render:test.
- Hook lines: "* " at the start of a line in lyrics.txt or after the time stamp in the .lrc; every line with the same words is a hook too. Hook lines can use their own font.
- Effects added 30 Oct 2026 (from the user's Canva finishing touches; src/effects/spread.ts, typewriter.ts, typing.ts, breathe.ts): **typewriter** is now letter by letter (typingPlan: from the moment the line appears (after the previous line has gone), one letter at a time at a steady pace = the intensity (subtle ~8, balanced ~14, expressive ~21 letters/s, TYPE_BASE_CPS 14 x the amount), raised just enough that every word is fully typed when it is sung (TYPE_MAX_CPS 90); a thin cursor in the accent colour after the last typed letter (takes no room), blinking softly (1.1 s) once the line is typed; fades out); **typewriter-erase**: the same, leaving by erasing its letters right to left over its exit while it dims; **typewriter-words**: the first typewriter (each word typed in 0.2 s before it is sung). Old manifests with "typewriter" render letter by letter now. **spread**: letters gathered close (-0.12 em, balanced) open up to their spacing while the line fades in (700 ms, ease-out), gather a little as it fades out; spacing never above 0, so never wider than the laid-out line. Title cards (Textboard): typewriter cards type at the calm pace and are complete a second before they end (typedShare), erase on the way out with typewriter-erase; spread opens their letter spacing. **Breathe** (recipe "breathe" off | gentle | beat, per platform, Style tab; props lyricEffects.breathe { mode, downbeats (video frames of the song's detected downbeats) }): an add-on to every effect, the line's scale x (1 + depth x (1 + cos) / 2), depth 2% verses / 3.5% hooks, "gentle" every 4 s, "beat" once a bar with the peak on each downbeat; not on the waiting next line; QA's safe-zone box counts it (effectExtents x BREATHE_MAX_SCALE). Style tab: "Set effect" panel (components/effect-preview.tsx): the song's first three lines (StyleData.sampleLines) drawn by LyricScene in a @remotion/player loop with any effect, intensity (shown as Typing pace calm / normal / quick for the typewriters) and Breathe; trying saves nothing.
- Effects added 30 Oct 2026, step 1 of the user's six (src/effects/clarify.ts, ascend.ts, ink.ts): **clarify** (word effect, moves like the verse effect): words still to come wait dim (35%) and softly blurred (4 px x the amount, at most 5), each pulls into focus over the 8 frames before it is sung (settled when sung). **ascend** (line effect, swap): while the line comes in (900 ms), its letters rise into place one after another (LineLook wave 0 -> 1; ascendLetter: 5 letters moving at once, from 28 px x the amount below, ease-out), in a wave across all its rows; settled at the usual time; fades out; QA's box counts the letters below (LyricEffect reach). **ink** (line effect, swap): uncovered left to right behind a soft-edged mask (inkMask, 12% edge), row after row, at the typewriter's pace x 1.3 (every word uncovered by the time it is sung); fades out. Title cards: ascend letters rise (WaveText), ink uncovers the card at the calm pace (done a second before it ends), clarify fades (no word times).
- Music-reactive add-ons, 30 Oct 2026 (src/effects/accents.ts; recipe heldGlow / chorusLift / shine "off" (default) | "on", per platform, Style tab with "Use for all my songs" = brand.json perPlatform on every platform with lyrics (setBrandAllPlatforms; the song's own value removed); props lyricEffects heldGlow / chorusLift / shine / downbeats; on any effect, never on the waiting next line): **held-note glow**: a word with its own aligned time of >= 0.8 s glows in the accent colour (up to 0.75; text tinted 35% toward the accent) rising over 70% of the hold, settling over 20 frames after; **chorus lift**: the first line of each chorus (a hook line after a non-hook) scales up to +5% with +0.3 glow, rising while it comes in, full when settled, eased back over 45 frames (QA's box counts it on hook lines); **shine**: one light sweep (20 frames, a white band clipped to the text) across each chorus line on the first downbeat at or after it is settled (else 15 frames after), not while a typewriter is still typing. "Set effect" has the three as checkboxes (the sample lines hold their last word ~1 s).
- Trending effects, 31 Oct 2026 (src/effects/trending.ts; LineLook rise / tiltX / tiltY / split / echo / ripple / rippleAmp / slice drawn by LyricScene): **word-pop** (word: each word bounces in, complete by its sung time; waiting words faint, hidden at expressive), **highlight-sweep** (word: fills with the accent word by word and stays lit), **mask-reveal** (slides up from behind its own bottom edge: clip-path + translate), **tilt-3d** (swings in on its horizontal axis, then a slight perspective sway), **glitch** (red / cyan split and jitter as it lands, a 4-frame glitch every ~2.6 s; `jumpy`: the smoothness test allows its sideways jumps), **wave** (letters ripple), **echo-stack** (faint copies stacked under the line, as text shadows), **slice** (cut in two, halves slide together), **kinetic-stack** (`kinetic`: one or two big words at a time on the voice, kineticGroups / kineticAt, fitted to 92% of the lyric width). Studio names: EFFECT_TITLES; gallery kinds: EFFECT_CATEGORIES. **Emphasis word** (recipe emphasis on, accents.ts emphasisWord): the word held longest (aligned times) else the longest word, never a small word; drawn in the hook font, 1.12x, in the accent.

## Fixing a line's words where it is shown (31 Oct 2026)
Shape → a lyric line's card → Text → Fix (lyrics-view.ts fixLyricLine): lyrics.txt gets the new words (the
old file in .studio\replaced\), and the stored alignment for the old words is copied to the key of the new
words with that line rewritten (lrc.ts retextLrcLine): same number of words = their own times; more or
fewer = spread evenly over the line's sung span; stamps, hook marker and end kept. The report and the
vocal-start check record are carried too; the line's hand-set start (hints.lrc "!" line) and its Textboard
card get the new words. Nothing is aligned again. Taps in hints.lrc are not touched (their hash is part of
the alignment key).
