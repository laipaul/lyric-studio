# Audio and video output

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). The music is never altered; fades, audio and video format, captions (.srt). Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Rule: Lyric Studio NEVER alters the music. The song file (song.wav|mp3) is only ever read (hashing, ffprobe, librosa analysis, Demucs input, the aligner, pnpm tap, Remotion's media server, the Lyrics tab's ▶, the QA comparison); nothing writes into it. It is only ever copied into the song folder, moved (migration), or moved to .studio\replaced\ when the user replaces it. Suno's vocal stems (the vocal stem and backing vocals in the song folder, under any names; formerly vocals.wav, backing-vocals.wav) are only ever read, never edited, never mixed back. They, the Demucs stems and the voice map are analysis only (workspace\cache, .studio\lyrics) and never go into any output. A video's audio is the song itself, cut to the section: a fade only where the music is cut (fadeInSeconds default 0.3 s, fadeOutSeconds per platform: standard / TikTok / Shorts 0.5 s, Reels 0.12 s for a tight loop; on Reels / TikTok / Shorts the automatic ending sets it (see Endings: a soft fade at least half a bar, up to 1 s, when it fits; also a ring-out bar on a part chosen by hand, 21 Nov 2026); never at the song's own start or end, so whole-song YouTube videos have no fades), AAC 320 kbps at the master's own sample rate (no resampling; platforms.json audioSampleRate 44100 / 48000 to force one), channels as Remotion outputs them (stereo; a mono master is the same sound on both). No volume change, loudness normalisation, EQ, compression or limiting (Remotion mixes with amix normalize=0 and only the fade curve); none is ever added unless the user sets it. QA "Audio matches the song" decodes the video's audio and the same section of the song file and WARNs when the length differs by more than 0.1 s, the level (outside the fades) by more than 0.5 dB, or the loudness curve (0.5 s blocks) by more than 1 dB on average. The manifest records settings.audio (fades, sample rate, resampled, bitrate, processing "none").
- Singing lines (G3, 21 Nov 2026, Paul's decision): for each line he ticks in Lyrics Sync → Singer, a piece of the vocal stem (lined up on the song by the stem check; else the song itself) is decoded into a temporary WAV and sent with a picture to WaveSpeed InfiniteTalk. The stem and the song are only read; the model's clip comes back with that voice, which is removed before the clip is kept (`-an`), so no file of ours and no video carries it. The video's audio stays the song, as always.
- Audio: the section start snaps to the nearest downbeat (report the adjusted time); a 0.3 s fade-in and a fade-out per platform preset (default 0.5 s; Reels 0.1-0.15 s, ending exactly on a bar, with the last shot continuing into the first clip for a seamless loop), only where the section cuts the music (see the rule above).
- Video: 30 fps, H.264, AAC 320 kbps at the master's own sample rate, yuv420p, faststart; size and video bitrate per platform preset.
- Every video on every platform gets a .srt (except the Spotify Canvas: no lyrics) with its lyric lines (times from the video's first frame).
