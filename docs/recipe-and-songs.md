# Recipe, clips of a run, anchors, instrumentals, migration

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). song.json inheritance and locks, collections of a run, story anchors, instrumental songs, pnpm make, migration. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Recipe song.json per song (fields verseEffect, hookEffect, verseFont, hookFont, accentColour, shotLength, tempo, collections, platforms, variations {platform: n}, section, length (target seconds for an auto section), ending (auto | loop | fade | ring-out), ep, youtubeLayout, perPlatform, locked [fields]). perPlatform (in song.json and brand.json): per-platform values for verseEffect, hookEffect, verseFont, hookFont, accentColour, section, length, shotLength, ending, e.g. { "youtube": { "verseEffect": "cinematic-fade" }, "reels": { "length": 90 } }. Inheritance for one platform (weakest first): library defaults -> artist brand (brand\<artist>\brand.json) -> artist brand perPlatform -> song.json -> song.json perPlatform -> this run (options / Generate; kept only with "save to song", which never overwrites a lock). platforms.json is format only (size, length, safe zones, text scale, bitrate, fades). A locked field uses only the song's own values (its default and its perPlatform values): a brand change does not reach it and a run cannot change it (the ignored choice is printed); locking copies the per-platform values seen at that moment. pnpm make prints a "recipe:" line with each value's source and one line per platform for per-platform values. Per-decision locks (a clip on a line, a word timing) come with 6a.
- Clips of a run = the song's own clips/ + its shared collections (merged; the same content in two folders is used once, with a warning). Collections: --collection a,b (or Generate's checkboxes), else song.json "collections", else the one remembered in .studio/settings.json (old songs). --collection (or clips of its own) is needed the first time; a --collection given on the command line is saved into song.json unless locked, so later runs may leave it out ("using collection wedding (remembered)"). A run from Lyric Studio never changes the song's collections (1 Nov 2026): only "Keep these choices for this song" does. A collection only remembered in .studio/settings.json is written into song.json by the Library's Tidy ("song.json gets collections from the collection its last run used"), so Shape shows what Make uses.
- Story line (11 Nov 2026): a song's shared collection gives only the clips its story line picked (clips.json "picked"), or all of them for a "collection:<name>" card at that place in "order"; a song from before is migrated to one card at the end (same videos). song.json "collections" lists the collections the line uses. A terminal run with --collection on a song with no picks or card still mixes the whole collection in, as before. Details: docs/screens.md (Storyboard: shared collections in the story line).
- Story anchors (song.json anchors [{ line, clip, clipIn?, transition? "cut" | "dissolve" }], set in the Lyrics tab; song-only, outside Reset/Lock; the line by its Lyrics tab number only: sung lines counted, blank lines not): the anchored clip cuts in on the downbeat at or just before the line's on-screen time and stays until at least the line's sung end; regular beat cuts fill the gaps; anchors win over the cut rate. Gaps in story order (file-name order, the other clips; an anchored clip never within 3 shots of its anchor) in every video when song.json gapOrder is "story" (default with anchors); story videos differ by style, cut rate and cut / dissolve; gapOrder "shuffle" = v1 story, the others shuffled. Dissolve ~1 bar centred on the downbeat. Per platform only anchors inside the section are used (the others listed); auto prefers the clean end that keeps the most anchors, after hook-first, the musical ending, loop-aware endings and never cutting a sung line. Conflicts (two anchors in one bar, an anchor that cannot hold its line, the same clip twice within 8 s, a line or clip not found, a clip slowed to cover its line) are a QA WARN "Story anchors" naming the lines and the clip, never dropped silently; pins.txt still works, an anchor wins (QA note). Manifest "anchors". Singing clips (G3, 21 Nov 2026): an anchor with "sync" (song seconds of the clip's first frame, set by Lyrics Sync → Singer) is a lip-sync clip: it never cuts in before its footage (the first beat it covers when it starts after the downbeat), plays at exactly normal speed from the frame that matches the song at its cut (clipIn, slips, speed and best moment ignored; its last frame holds if it runs out), always with a hard cut (no dissolve, also with transitions "dissolve"), and is never used to fill other shots (shots.ts anchorOnly / exact); the other shots keep the story order. QA "Singer in time" lists the lines where the singer is in time and names a singing line a video leaves out. Singing blocks (21 Nov 2026): lines in a row sung from the same Creation picture or video (no skipped line, at most 8 s of music between them; node/singer.ts singingRuns) are one take, anchored on the first line with "through" = the last line: it holds until the last line is sung (anchors.ts throughLyric), then the regular cuts take over.
- Instrumental songs (song.json "instrumental": true; "+ New song" Instrumental; src/story.ts, storyAlignment in node/song-project.ts): songPaths().lyricsTxt is the song folder's story.txt (paths.instrumental), optional (empty or missing = music only). Never aligned, no vocal-start check: the story (one sentence per line, a blank line = a pause of 2 bars) is timed by the app: each sentence shown exactly on a bar (stamp = bar + lead), whole bars covering its reading time (1.5 s + 0.35 s a word, at least 3 s; "sung" until read, so it fades before a pause), 2 bars of intro and outro, the spare bars spread evenly so the story runs through the piece; too long: pauses, then extra time go first, the sentences that do not fit are said. Stored as .studio\lyrics\story-<hash>.lrc (Alignment source "story"), so the Lyrics tab (nudges, hand-set starts; editor = story.txt), Storyboard, Textboard and videos use it like lyrics. Short-form sections start at the strongest stretch of the music (strongestStretch: the 4-bar phrase whose target length is loudest by RMS; anchor "music"; moved back before a sentence it would cut), endings as usual; pacing calm ("an instrumental, paced as a calm piece"); every story sentence starts a new shot (cuts cutAtMs on its bar). No .lrc for lyric services; .srt only with a story; the title opening without a story stays 4.5 s.
- `pnpm make <song folder>`: song, lyrics and hints.lrc come from the folder (the files linked in .studio\files.json under any names; else song.wav|mp3 or the only audio file that is not a stem by its name, lyrics.txt or the only other .txt).
- Migration: `pnpm songs:migrate` = dry run (changes nothing); `--apply` asks "Make sure your regular backup is recent before continuing (y/n)", backs up studio.db + settings + a journal to workspace/migrations/<date>/, moves projects/<id> -> songs/<artist>/<title> and clips/<name> -> collections/<name>, writes lyrics.txt / hints.lrc / song.json, keeps the database consistent and records the moves (old manifests are never edited and still re-render identically); `--undo` (app stopped) reverses the last apply. The Library banner's Preview / Migrate do the same. A song is built in songs/.migrating-<id>/ (new songs: songs/.adding-<id>/) and renamed once at the end; its database folder is set only then, so an interrupted run is finished by the next one and a song is never split. --apply also merges songs split over two folders into the plain name (never overwriting: a clash keeps both), and repairs missing lyrics.txt (lyrics of the newest video's manifest), hints.lrc (stored hints) and empty song.json (settings.json or a migration backup); the dry run lists every merge, repair and song.json without settings (with the reason). A folder with .studio/ or song.json counts as made by Lyric Studio.
- Planned slots (C2, 18 Nov 2026): an imported shot list (shotlist.json) puts "slot:<id>" entries in clips.json "order". They are not clips: Make, Shape, the clip order (rankOf / inClipOrder) and the anchors ignore them until a slot has a picture (C3). A slot's "line" becomes a story anchor only then.
