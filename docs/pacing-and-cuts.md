# Pacing, transitions, cuts and clip order

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). Automatic pacing and transitions, shot length, tempo, cut grid, clip order, shuffle, pins, sources. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Automatic pacing (src/pace.ts; recipe "shotLength" "auto", the library default): the song's feel from how densely it is sung (words per second over the sung stretches; < 2.2 calm, < 3.2 medium, else driving; never from the tempo alone) and the clips' usable length (median of in/out with speed; a planned shot never needs a clip slower than 0.8x): target shots calm 6 s verses / 3.5 s hooks, medium 3.5 / 2, driving 2 / 1.5, in whole bars (any number 1-8; odd counts above 2 only when clearly better), hooks never longer than verses; **normal speed first** (2 Nov 2026): a shot longer than the typical usable clip costs a slow-motion penalty (SLOW_WEIGHT 2 x ln(shot / clip) added to the bar's score; the 0.8x limit stays the hard stop), so 5.2 s clips at 152 BPM get 3-bar verse shots (4.7 s, normal speed) instead of 4 bars (6.3 s) slowed to 0.82x; slow motion is the user's creative choice (clip editor "speed"), never a side effect of pacing; a clip the user slowed counts as longer; the cut plan uses it like "story" (hook / verse bars on downbeats, never a short last shot). A --rate given on the command line uses the cut rate instead; shotLength "rate" = the cut rate (Generate's 0.5x / 1x / 2x). Storyboard header + pnpm make: "Pacing: a calm song (2.0 words/s) and clips of about 5.2 s → 3 bars (5.3 s) on verses, 2 bars (3.6 s) on hooks."; manifest settings.pace.
- Transition handles (22 Nov 2026, src/handles.ts fitHandles): every dissolve (automatic or anchored) is fitted to the frames the clips really have inside the user's in / out points, so no frame is ever frozen: first the clip slides inside its kept part (the shot keeps its length on screen, the cut stays on the beat), then it is slowed at most 10% (never a singing clip, a loop shot or a clip already held), else that dissolve is shortened, down to a hard cut. What changed is in the shot plan (ShotProps.fitNotes) and shows on the Storyboard clip cards (node/transition-notes.ts) and in Shape's clip list (StoryboardClip.transitionNotes). Replaces src/blend.ts (2c2c863, same day). Into and out of a singing (lip-sync) clip (28 Nov 2026, Paul: "the transition into singing clips is harsh again"; replaces the 22 Nov fade over the take's own lead-in, which only worked when a take happened to have lead-in): a singing clip is locked to the song at 1.00x and never moved, slowed, frozen or faded, so the softness comes from the B-roll beside it. **In**: the shot before plays on past the cut (its `tail`, `ShotProps.tailOver`, drawn on top) and dissolves out over the singer's first 0.4 s (SOFT_SINGING_FRAMES, 12 frames); the singer is fully there underneath from its cut frame, and the cut stays on its beat. **Out**: the shot after starts 0.4 s early (a normal `dissolve {before: 12, after: 0}`) and fades in over the singer's last 0.4 s. The same for every singing source (an InfiniteTalk take, an MV Generator take, a shot of a music video, a singing clip over a Mini film, the singer coming back after a hidden moment: the cut-away dissolves out over the returning singer), because they are all anchors with a sync time (compose.ts softIn / softOut; HandleShot.singingIn / fadeInBeforeCut). Footage the B-roll lacks: it slides inside the part the user kept or slows at most 10% as for any dissolve, else the dissolve is shortened to what it has (under 0.2 s a hard cut with a note: "a hard cut into this singing take (the clip before it has no footage left to play on)" / "a hard cut out of this singing take (this clip has no footage before its start)"); never a frozen frame. Still a hard cut for the clip's own "comes in with: cut", the crisp style or an all-cuts variation, and between two singing clips. Remotion (LyricVideo TailFadeOut) and HyperFrames (compile.ts) draw it from the same props; lyric timing, the cuts and the audio are not touched; plan fingerprint v 4 (make.ts: old shot plans are planned again once, the old one kept as .previous.json). Tests: test/singing-soft-transitions.test.ts, handles, anchors.
- Automatic transitions (src/transitions.ts; recipe "transitions" auto (default) | crisp (cuts) | flowing (dissolves outside the hooks), per platform; --transitions; a clip's own "comes in with" cut / dissolve in the Storyboard clip editor = clips.json "transition", always wins; anchored shots keep their own): auto = cuts on the hooks; calm songs dissolve (about 1.5 s in whole beats, centred on the cut, compose.ts autoDissolveFrames; Paul's Canva length, 22 Nov 2026) in verses, intros and instrumental passages; medium songs dissolve only where nobody sings; driving songs cut; a short dip to black (half a bar, 8-18 frames, darkest on the cut, under the lyrics) where a verse opens into a chorus (calm and medium; driving cuts). Props dips[]; Storyboard header line "Transitions: …" and ● dip / ◐ dissolve badges; manifest settings.transition_style and transition_plan {text, kinds}.
- Shot length (recipe "shotLength": brand / song / per platform, Style tab + Generate, --shot-length): "auto" (above), 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars per shot (energy low doubles, high halves; counted from the bar the section starts on), or "story" = 2 bars on hooks, 4 on verses and instrumentals, more bars while a shot is under 3 s, never a short last shot; set, it replaces the cut rate. Unset = the cut rate.
- Tempo override (song.json "tempo", Style tab: typed, ½ ×2 ×2/3 ×3/2, Tap test, "Check the tempo" = the tempo candidates; --tempo): the analysis re-tracks the beats around it; pnpm make prints it with the detected one; manifest settings.tempo. `pnpm tempo:check "<song folder>"` lists the likeliest tempi with their ratio to the detected one.
- Cut points come from beats inside the selected section. Cut rate 1x = cut every downbeat (every bar), 2x = every half bar, 0.5x = every 2 bars, Off = one clip per lyric line.
- Every cut lands earlyCutFrames (default 2, range 0-4, at 30 fps) before its beat. Section energy low halves the cut rate, high doubles it. Optional holdOnLyricLines: never cut inside a lyric line; cut at the next line start, snapped to the nearest beat.
- Minimum shot length 0.8 s. If the grid is too fast, step to the next coarser REGULAR grid (every beat -> every 2 beats -> every bar ...) instead of dropping single cuts.
- A clip shorter than its shot is slowed down to fit (playbackRate, minimum 0.5x); only if 0.5x is still too short, its last frame is held. The pool prefers clips that cover the shot at their own speed first, so this is the last resort.
- **The user's clip edits stay exact** (2 Nov 2026): a shot never plays outside a clip's in / out, and a clip with a speed set plays at exactly that speed whenever it covers the shot. Only when a trimmed or slowed clip is shorter than its shot and no other clip covers it does the planner slow it further (or hold its last frame), because moving the cut to where the clip ends would change the cuts the lyrics, anchors, dissolves and the loop shot are built on. The Shot panel then says what creative input fixes it: "your trimmed clip (2.5 s) is shorter than this 4.7 s shot: lengthen it in Edit clip or slow it down there".
- **Held-frame notice** (2 Nov 2026): a held last frame shorter than 0.2 s (HOLD_NOTICE_MS in storyboard.ts) cannot be seen: no "the clip is too short" warning and no hold flag on the shot (a clip one frame short is common and harmless).
- Since 2 Nov 2026 (the user's decision) **every video of a run follows the song's clip order** (set in Assets or Shape → Clips, one sequence in clips.json); there is no automatic shuffle any more (song.json "gapOrder" is no longer read; the Lyrics tab's gap-order choice is gone); the videos differ by style, cut rate and cut / dissolve. A clip set to **Omit** (clips.json "clips": {"x.mp4": {"omit": true}}, the Use / Omit switch in Assets and Shape) keeps its place in the order but is never shown; an anchor on an omitted clip becomes a slot ("needs a clip"); every clip omitted is a plain error.
- Clip order: "story" (default) = clips in the story order (clips.json "order", else file-name order: 01_, 02_, ...; numbers compared as numbers), every clip once before any repeats (rounds), a clip's first shot centred on its best moment (clips.json "best", else the strongest 2 s, workspace\cache\best-moments.json) inside its in / out (clips.json "in" / "out"; "speed" 0.5-1 slows it), a clip shown again continues where it stopped; an anchor without clipIn is centred on the best moment too; "shuffle" = seeded random.
- Shuffle is DETERMINISTIC: same seed + same inputs = same clip sequence. Use a seeded PRNG (e.g. mulberry32). Avoid the same clip twice in a row, and avoid the last 3 clips when the pool allows.
- Pins (pins.txt: "lyric line = clip file") force a clip onto the shot where that line appears on screen, at every occurrence; pins override the order mode. Lyric lines are matched ignoring case, punctuation and extra spaces. Warn when a line or clip is not found.
- Clips are cover-cropped to 9:16 and start at a random (seeded) offset inside the clip.
- Sources by orientation: 9:16 uses vertical (and square) sources, horizontal ones only if at least 2x the output height; 16:9 uses horizontal sources. Too few: QA WARN with the count needed; blurred fill only with --allow-fill. Photos (jpg/png/webp) are valid sources with a slow seeded Ken Burns move (zoom 1.0-1.12, gentle pan) fitted to the shot.
- Preview and render must use the exact same composition and props.
- **Cuts and clip starts set by hand (7 Nov 2026, src/timeline-edits.ts):** the Shape timeline's roll (a cut moved, on the beat unless "free") and slip (the part of the clip a shot plays) are kept in the song's clips.json as "cuts" [{ from, to, free? }] and "slips" [{ at, clip, in }] in song seconds, so they apply to every video covering that moment. The planner (compose.ts) applies the cut moves after the automatic cuts and the story anchors (a hand-moved cut is final; never closer than 0.4 s to a neighbouring cut; one whose cut is not in the plan is left aside), then the slips: **a slip never decides which clip plays** (the story order does, 8 Nov 2026); it applies only while the order still puts the slip's clip in that shot (and on an anchored shot only with the anchor's clip), so a slip left over from before a reorder in Assets or Shape is left aside and the clips follow the user's order. New clips added in Assets go at the end of the order; the order is one sequence for Assets, Shape and Make. A roll also writes the slips that keep the left shot's first frame and the right shot's last frame.
- **Takes ("Make another", 11 Nov 2026).** A take is one more video of a platform made from the song's own settings. Its variation number (take N: `planTake(N)`, the number in the file name, v3.mp4) is separate from the name the user sees: the studio sends the next variation number no file of that platform uses yet (one more than the highest file number in any run), and the video that comes out is called the next song-wide version ("Reels v4", docs/screens.md "Version names"). In plain words, what varies between takes: your clip order, the cuts (the song's beats and cut rate), the lyric timing, the music and the ending are the song's and are never shuffled or changed by a take. Before, a take differed only in style (Simple / Bold), cinematic bars (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) and, from take 3, the cut rate and cut / dissolve; on a short song, with a look that makes Simple and Bold alike, or on YouTube's cover layout (no clips on screen, so nothing else to vary) that was close to nothing: three near-identical videos. Now a take from take 2 on also varies, deterministically from its number (`moment = take - 1`, `planTake` in make.ts): (1) which part of each clip its first shot plays (seeded by the take and the clip's place in the story order, always inside the clip's in / out; not on a clip whose best moment the user set, nor on an anchor, pin, slip or loop shot; `assignClips` option `moment`); (2) on the YouTube cover layout, the cover's camera move (take 2 pulls out, 3 holds, 4 pushes in, ...; `coverZoomFor`). Take 1 is exactly v1; a run of N videos (not a take) is unchanged. Effect intensity and fonts are the user's choices and are not varied. **Warning before a duplicate** (the same check for both ways of making a video): before queuing, POST /api/runs plans what would be made (a dry plan in the planner process, `takeProblem` for a take and `duplicateProblem` / `--dup-check` for a normal Make run, both in take-check.ts) and compares it with the platform's videos that are not discarded (a try counts too): the props a render would get (the shot plan as Make would settle it, nothing saved) against the props in each made video's manifest; the cover's slow seeded drift does not count as a difference. If one is equal: a take answers 400 "Reels v4 would be identical to Reels v1 (made 11 Nov 15:05): change a setting in Shape first (e.g. choose another look, add another clip or photo, set a clip's best moment or length)"; a normal Make run ("Make N videos", "One of each", the play button of a chip) answers 409 `{ error: "Reels v4 would be identical to Reels v3 (made 11 Nov 15:05): change a setting in Shape first, or press Make anyway.", duplicate: true }` and queues nothing, and Make shows the message with a **Make anyway** button (data-make-anyway) that sends the same request with `force: true`. Not checked: Compare looks, pictures, fonts, themes, a platform with no video yet, the Canvas. A check that cannot run lets the run through.
- **Mini film: shot by shot** (23 Nov 2026, Step B; node/film-plan.ts): for a story song (make.ts isWholeStory) every card of the story line is one shot in that order, replacing the automatic pacing, the cut rate, story anchors and gap filling (singing clips on their lines stay: since 27 Nov 2026, Paul "yes over the film", a singing take or music-video shot on its lines plays over the film there, in time with the song, like a performance cut with the story around it; the film's shots fill the rest by time, compose.ts, and a singing clip is no story card of its own, film-plan.ts; a clip of the other shape, e.g. a 16:9 music video in the Film 9:16, is not used and the film says so): a card on a lyric line (its slot's line in shotlist.json, else its clip's non-singing anchor) is cut on the beat at or just before the line appears (at most 0.2 s after), cards without a line share the time of the card before them (shot-grid.ts slotSpans); each take plays once (RenderBase.filmShots pins every shot; the variations keep the cuts); planned shots without a take show a stand-in (cache/standins, the first person's reference picture dimmed and blurred, else a dark frame; "Shot N · no take yet"); a card without room is left out with a note, and a card on a line wins over a card without one just before it. Canvas keeps its loop.
