# YouTube and Spotify Canvas

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). The full-song YouTube video and the Canvas loop. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- YouTube (16:9, full song from 0:00 so the intro is kept): layouts "cover" (default: --cover, else the project's cover, else cover.jpg/cover.png next to the song; else stills, else clips, with a message), "stills" (one horizontal picture per lyric section from pauses and hooks, stills.txt or name order, 1.5 s crossfade on the downbeat nearest each section start, slow Ken Burns, subtle grain and a beat-pulsed glow, switchable) and "clips" (beat-cut montage of horizontal sources). Title + artist in the brand title font (Cinzel, wide spacing, accent colour): small at the top in the cover layout (centred over the lyrics column), over the opening seconds (gone by ~4 s) otherwise; no separate title card. Logo from WORKSPACE_DIR\brand. Cover layout: cover 0.672 of the height, active lyric 1.18x and next line 1.1x the 5g.2 sizes, zoom 1.00-1.08 with a gentle seeded drift and the background moving 30% the opposite way (parallax). Section mood on YouTube: a warm, light veil on hook (chorus) sections and a soft dark veil on verses, 2 s eased changes centred on the downbeat nearest each section start, never flashing. A full-song video must be as long as the song (QA "Video length"). The app chooses the end-screen window itself (endScreenLayout): it starts when the last lyric line has left (+ 0.5 s) but is at least 5 s (YouTube's minimum) and at most 20 s (platforms.json endScreen.seconds), the full 20 s without lyrics; props endScreen {fromFrame, seconds}. The bottom-right end-screen area stays clear in it; lyrics move to a band above only when the singing reaches into the window (the last 5 s at the latest): the lyric area then glides there (eased, 24 frames before the window; lines that end earlier are untouched), never a jump. The logo is gone by the window's start; publish.txt tells the length to set in YouTube Studio ({{endScreen}}: "End screen: the last 14 s (from 3:41)", appended as an "== End screen ==" note after the tags for templates without it). Each YouTube video gets a 1280x720 thumbnail (brand title font, logo) and publish.txt (title "<Song> (Lyrics) - Life Lyrics", description with an optional "From the EP <name>" line (--ep), the full lyrics, credits, Spotify placeholder, hashtags, tags) from WORKSPACE_DIR\brand\publish-template.txt.
- Spotify Canvas (--platforms canvas): 9:16, a seamless loop of the longest whole number of bars in 3-8 s (whole beats only at very slow tempos), the strongest visual moment (FFmpeg motion/colour/contrast score, no hard scene change inside), or the clip (and moment) chosen in the Storyboard clip editor ("Use for Canvas (from here)" = song.json "canvas": { clip, at }; "let the app choose" removes it; a chosen clip too short even slowed falls back to the others, said so); the longest loop first: a clip at normal speed, else slowed gently (>= 0.75x) for that length, then fewer bars (at 1x or gently slowed), then 2) the shortest loop slowed down (>= 0.6x), 3) a vertical photo with slow motion, only then an error naming what would work; the fallback used is printed; NO lyrics, NO text, NO logo, NO audio; the last 0.5 s blends into the clip frames just before the start (photos: a Ken Burns move out and back). QA: Seamless loop, No audio, Length 3-8 s.
