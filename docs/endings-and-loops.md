# Sections, endings and loops

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). Musical endings, target length, platform sections, clean cuts, endings, loops, loop picture. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Principle: a musical ending beats an exact length. Never trade a clean line / phrase ending for hitting a target duration; the tolerance exists for that. Every length or trimming feature (auto sections, target lengths, loops, EP films, future trims) must follow this.
- Automatic length (2 Nov 2026, the default: recipe length empty; Shape → Automatic → Length "Automatic"): target presets with length.auto (platforms.json: Reels 45-120 s near 90, TikTok 15-45 near 25, Shorts 30-75 near 50) keep the +-15% window around the target (chorus lines first) and otherwise take the nearer of a shorter and a longer finished phrase anywhere in the auto range. Said in plain words as the app's choice (SectionChoice.lengthText, manifest settings.length {text, advice}, the plan's decisions): "Length: automatic, 1:48: ends after the chorus line ...". A typed length keeps the rules below; when it ends shorter or longer, the line says so and the Shape page offers "Use automatic". QA "Length for the platform" (fixed sections) uses the auto max as the limit.
- Target length (length, --length, Generate's "Target length"): shapes an "auto" section only, per platform: a target of that length with the preset's tolerance (hook-first start, ends after a complete line, preferably a chorus line, never cutting a sung line; up to 25% shorter preferred over longer). Longer than the song: the whole song, said so. Not used for YouTube (whole song) or Canvas (loop). A fixed section (0:11-1:10) stays exactly as given; longer than the platform allows (target + 30%, or the range's hard maximum) is a QA WARN "Length for the platform", never trimmed.
- Platform sections: short-form (reels, tiktok, shorts) is hook-first: start at most 1 bar before the first hook line (hook heard within ~2.5 s; if a line is sung right into the hook so no bar in reach is clean, the start moves back to the bar before that line appears, at most 2 lines back, and the hook comes later: a clean start beats the 2.5 s), then extend forward to the target length. Length: within +-15% of the target; if impossible, the nearest clean line end up to 25% shorter is preferred over a longer one (up to 30%); report which. A section that cuts a sung line is a QA WARN naming the line, never PASS. Every song also gets a clean synced .lrc (for Musixmatch / LyricFind) next to the videos.
- Clean cuts: a line's singing ends at its last word's end time (word timings from the aligned LRC: `pnpm align` writes a trailing end tag per line). A cut is clean at any bar boundary after the last word ends and before the next line's first word. Only without word timings: line-start logic (a line lasts until the next starts).
- Section ends: on a bar; TikTok and Shorts may end on any beat when no bar is clean (bars preferred); Reels keeps bar ends for the seamless loop.
- Endings (src/ending.ts; Reels, TikTok, Shorts; recipe "ending", per platform: auto (default) | loop | fade | ring-out; Style tab, --ending): auto = the app chooses from the music at the section's end: a tight loop (cut on the bar with the platform's short fade) only when the end flows back into the start (whole bars, a key that fits both bars, and a level jump clearly under 3 dB: the worst within a frame either side of the start and end, with a 0.75 dB margin, so QA on the rendered frames agrees); else a soft fade over the room after the last sung word, starting at the earliest halfway into a held last note (>= 0.6 s; never on the words) (at most one bar, at most 3 s, at least 0.3 s); with less than 3/4 bar of room, a ring-out: one more bar (the next detected bar line, no sung line in it, within the platform's hard maximum, auto sections only) faded over that bar; at the song's own end no fade. A requested ring-out that does not fit becomes a fade with a note. Whole bars kept, no sung line cut, the music never changed (only the fade curve). With a fade / ring-out, QA "Loop flows" counts a level jump or key change as softened (a note, not a WARN). The Storyboard header shows the ending in plain words; pnpm make prints it; manifest settings.ending {kind, fade_out_s, text}. Loop suggestions never drop a chorus line or the title line (its words in order) the plan has, and never repeat the plan's own end.
- Loops (platforms.json "loops": reels, tiktok, shorts; never YouTube or Canvas): QA "Loop flows" checks the wrap-around (src/loop.ts): beat (length = whole bars counted on the song's detected downbeats, start and end each within 50 ms of a detected bar line; the average BPM is only the fallback without downbeats), phrase (ends after a complete line, starts at most 1 bar before a sung line; with the title opening on, up to the section's own title lead-in instead: 2 bars, or 1 when 2 bars > 5 s, section.ts titleLeadMs, passed to loopFlows by make.ts for the manifest, the Storyboard and QA alike; the auto end chooser and the Storyboard's loop suggestions only look at the end, beat, level and harmony, so they agree), sound (the song's last 100 ms vs first 100 ms of the section, WARN > 3 dB), harmony (chroma of the last and first bar against the 24 keys, WARN when no key fits both, below 0.15), picture (same clip first and last, except the Reels continuation); each failing point names its cause; the manifest records "loop". The auto end picks, among the clean line ends inside the tolerance (chorus first), the one that flows best back into the start, and says why ("loop: ..."). Shuffle never puts the first shot's clip on the last shot. A first shot whose start is set by hand (a slip on the Shape timeline, or an anchor's clipIn) keeps it: the Reels loop ending then uses only the frames before it (else an ordinary last shot), and a last shot with a hand-set start plays from it as an ordinary last shot, not as the loop shot.
- Title opening with a part of the song that starts on (or within 2.5 s of) a sung line (chosen by hand; the auto section leaves titleLead): titleCards (src/node/textboard.ts) keeps a card block that does not touch the first line's box on screen for TITLE_MIN_S (2.5 s); a block that does is shown alone until the line fades in, and a note (plan decisions + warnings) names the creative fix (start the part about 2 bars earlier, or Automatic). Never silent, the music and lyric timing are never touched.
- Loop picture (Reels, TikTok, Shorts): when the last shot does not continue into the first, the last 0.4-1 s fade into the first shot's opening frames (the frames just before it, else its first frame held): props loopInto {frames}; the "no seamless loop ending" warning only without it; QA "Loop flows" picture counts it as continuing.

**Finished phrases (1 Nov 2026).** An automatic section ends only after a finished phrase: not after a line
ending in a comma, dash, colon or "and/but/or", and inside a chorus only after its last line (the chorus is
every occurrence of the most repeated block that holds a hook line, not only the marked line). The length
tiers stay: in the window, else up to 25% shorter, else up to 30% longer. Only when no finished phrase is in
reach at all does any clean line end count.
- **Soundtrack window (7 Nov 2026):** on the Shape page the user can drag the part of the song a video uses on the whole song: the start snaps to a bar, the end to the first bar after a sung line (src/section-window.ts cleanEnds / snapWindow), else a bar; saved as that platform's fixed section in song.json (perPlatform "section"), "Automatic" sets it back to auto. A fixed section is used exactly as given (start snapped to the nearest bar), as with "Part of the song".
