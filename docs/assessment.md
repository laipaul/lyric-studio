# Pro assessment and reactions

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). The verdict per video and the React buttons. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Pro assessment (src/assess.ts; Step C): every video gets one verdict, ✅ Ready to post / 🟡 Good — N things to improve / 🔴 Not ready — fix N things first, built from its QA warnings (v<N>.qa.txt) and manifest (slow-motion shots below 0.75x, the Canvas loop shorter than possible): at most 3 points shown, each in plain words with the creative action that fixes it (e.g. "Line 22 … may start a little early or late" → "Listen to it in the Lyrics tab (▶), then nudge it or mark it Checked by ear"); majors (a sung line cut by the section, lyrics in the safe zone, black frames, music not matching the song, wrong length, Canvas loop / audio / length, too long for the platform) make it Not ready; "Handled by the app" lists the pacing / transitions / ending decisions; the QA lines one click away ("Show the technical checks"). Learning: a minor point (same code, e.g. "review:22") on videos the user approved at least twice no longer counts ("You usually accept"). Videos tab: the verdict badge replaces the QA badge, "★ strongest" marks the clearly strongest video of a platform's run (fewest points, majors weigh 10). Storyboard header: the same assessment predicted from the plan ("(prediction before rendering)": loop, anchors, clips repeated within 3 shots, lines flagged for review, slow motion).
- Reactions (src/reactions-list.ts, src/node/reactions.ts; Step C): keyword buttons under each video ("React"): cuts too fast / too slow (recipe paceShift ±1 bar on the automatic pacing; an explicit shot length steps 1/2/4/8), too many dissolves (transitions crisp), more dreamy (flowing), ending too abrupt (ending ring-out; loop platforms), lyrics hard to read (readability strong), too repetitive (paceShift +1, and "add clips"), love this one (favourite + approved, the video's transitions / ending / effects kept in song.json). ("This clip doesn't fit here" on a Storyboard shot was removed on 2 Nov 2026: the shot's "Omit this clip" does the job; a stale one in an old log or request is refused, never a crash.) Each first shows "Next run: …" (or "Nothing to change: …"), then applies to "This song" (song.json; a per-platform value of the song for that platform that would hide it is removed) or "All my songs" (brand.json perPlatform, every platform except Canvas; ending: loop platforms only). Logged in the song's .studio\feedback.jsonl. API POST /api/songs/[id]/reaction { video, reaction, clip?, scope?, apply? }. Recipe fields paceShift (-3..+4 bars; the shift may slow clips to 0.65x; the Pacing line says "your adjustment: +1 bar") and readability (auto | normal | strong: props.lyricBacking, a soft dark gradient band behind the lyric area, under the lyrics).
- Lyric readability "auto" (default since U1; src/readability.ts pure, src/node/lyric-band.ts measuring): for each video with lyrics over clips (not the Canvas, not the YouTube cover / stills layouts) the render measures every clip of the video behind the lyric band (FFmpeg, a 36x64 grey frame at the clip's best moment, else 1 s in; the 90th percentile, as relative luminance; cached in workspace\cache\lyric-band.json) and checks the white lyric colour and the hook accent against it (WCAG 3:1, thin strokes — weight ≤ 300 or a thin script — 4.5:1). When at least a quarter of the measured clips (at least one) are too bright, the soft backing goes behind the lyrics on the whole video. The log and the manifest (settings.readability: mode, backing, clips_measured, clips_bright, text) say which and why, e.g. "lyric readability auto: 2 of 8 clips are bright behind the lyrics, so a soft dark band goes behind them". "strong" (always) and "normal" (never) stay the user's overrides (Style → Automatic → Lyric readability; the reaction "lyrics hard to read" sets strong). The music is never touched.
