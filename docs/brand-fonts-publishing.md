# Brand, fonts and publishing texts

_Part of the project brief (CLAUDE.md links here). brand.json and logo, fonts, font pairings, artist per project, captions for posting. Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 30 Oct 2026; keep it up to date when this area changes._

- Brand: WORKSPACE_DIR\brand\brand.json (title font Cinzel, verse font Cormorant Garamond, hook font Kaushan Script, accent colour, logo corners and size) + logo.png. Logo: only on the platforms in brand.json logo.platforms (default ["youtube"]: no logo on reels, tiktok, shorts, canvas or other vertical presets); YouTube bottom-right by default (logo.youtube), fading out (0.5 s) before the last-20-s end-screen window; 9:16 when enabled: logo.vertical ("auto" = bottom right). Always anchored to a frame corner with a fixed margin (3% of the width), never mid-frame (QA "Logo in a frame corner"), never over the lyrics (QA). Lyrics use the brand fonts; --font/--font-hook override. Thumbnails and title cards use the brand title font.
- Fonts: bundled @fontsource families (fonts/LICENSES.md; incl. Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Great Vibes, EB Garamond, Spline Sans, Space Mono, Bitter, Grand Hotel, Lato, Abril Fatface, Poppins, Arima, Mulish, Special Elite, Noto Sans JP / Serif JP: CJK families load only the pieces the lyrics use) plus any .ttf/.otf/.woff/.woff2 in WORKSPACE_DIR\fonts (D:\videomaker\workspace\fonts; shown in the Style tab); props list the exact font files, so preview and render load the same ones. Weight and italic per font (recipe verseWeight / hookWeight 100-900, verseItalic / hookItalic; brand.json verseFont/hookFont/titleFont weight + italic; per platform; Reset and Lock): the exact file, else the nearest weight (upright when no italic) with a warning naming it; text is drawn with font-synthesis: none (never a fake bold or italic). The recipe's weight applies to every variation (Simple and Bold differ in case, shadow, animation and size). The manifest records settings.fonts (family, weight, italic, pairing).
- Font pairings (presets/font-pairings.json: verse + hook (+ optional title) font with weight and italic, a note, an optional warning; "Current brand" = the artist's brand.json fonts, first): Shape → Style → Look shows them as a grid of compact cards (components/pairings-row.tsx; 2-5 per row by width, never a horizontal scroll), each a small picture of the pair together on the song's own verse and hook line, as the video draws them (PairPreview: layoutLyric, size, weight, italic, shadow, karaoke accent), over a frame of the song's clips (else its cover), and its name ("brand" chip, "try this" for the recommended pair). **Clicking a card chooses it for this song** (song.json; locked fields kept, named); the chosen card has a ✓ and the brand-coloured border (pink is reserved for it). The description and the exact faces ("verse Cormorant Garamond 400 · hook … 600 italic") show on hover / keyboard focus; "Save to brand…" is a small link on hover / focus and always on the chosen card, and asks first (writes brand.json, with the title font); "Back to the brand" resets the song's fonts. Notes per pair (src/node/pairings.ts pairingNotes: each in plain words + the technical detail): missing é è ç à ô, characters of the song's lyrics a font lacks (e.g. Japanese) — both need a creative choice (another pair); low contrast over the frame (3:1, thin strokes 4.5:1); a thin script on hook lines over 24 characters; the pair's own warning. They are not shown on the cards (a non-chosen card only has a tiny muted "!" with the technical text as tooltip): under the grid, the **chosen** pair's notes in plain words (e.g. "Lyrics may be hard to read over bright clips like this one: the app adds a soft backing behind them when your clips need it"), the ratios under "Details". Readability is the app's job: with Lyric readability "auto" (the default) the render adds the soft backing by itself (see docs/assessment.md); the line says so, or that the backing is on ("strong"), or that "normal" switched it off. Compare: Generate's "Compare font pairings" (2-3) or pnpm make --pairings a,b: one video per pairing with the same section, clips, order and style.
- Artist per project (default "Life Lyrics"; --artist or the New song form): titles, thumbnails, overlays and publish texts use it; its brand is workspace\brand\<artist>\brand.json when present, else workspace\brand\ (Life Lyrics). The EP is stored with the project too (--ep or the form) and reused.
- Captions for posting per artist and platform: brand\<artist>\publish-<platform>.txt (e.g. Pômé in French) else brand\publish-<platform>.txt (English defaults created the first time; placeholders {{song}} {{artist}} {{ep}} {{epLine}} {{hook}} {{songTag}} {{artistTag}} {{year}}); YouTube uses the publish.txt next to the video (its template: brand\<artist>\publish-template.txt else brand\publish-template.txt). Canvas has no caption.
